Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. Last updated 28 November 2026. This policy covers consumer health data under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws in Nevada, Connecticut and other states. HomeCircle is run by Luke Endrody (sole trader), Queensland, Australia, ABN 53 687 374 897. Contact: support@homecircle.au (not a crisis line).

In short

The site has no accounts, forms, cookies, ads, trackers or analytics, and nothing you save on the site is sent to us. Two things can still involve information that may say something about your health: the technical records our web host receives when you open a page, and any email you choose to send us. We don't sell consumer health data and we don't use it for ads.

What may be collected, and why

  • Visit records (collected by our web host). Your IP address, device and browser information, the page or file you asked for, the date and time, and the page that linked you here if your browser sends it. Because this is a recovery website, the page you visit (for example, a page about alcohol, drugs, gambling or overdose) could suggest something about your health. Purpose: to deliver the page to you and to keep the site secure. We don't use these records for anything else. GitHub also describes other uses of website-usage information under GitHub's privacy statement, which GitHub, not HomeCircle, controls. We don't routinely see individual visitor logs and we don't keep our own visitor-log database. GitHub hasn't published how long it keeps these records for GitHub Pages.
  • Emails you send us. Your email address, your name if you include it, what you write (including any health information you choose to share) and standard email details such as the date. Purpose: to reply to you and to handle any request you make under this policy. We delete support emails within 90 days after our last reply, unless you ask us to keep the conversation open or the law requires us to keep it. For each request under this policy we keep a short record (the date, the type of request and what we did, without your health details) for 24 months, so we can show we handled it.

The "hide breath holds" setting stays in your browser on your device. It doesn't record any health condition, and we never receive it.

We don't collect any other consumer health data. If we ever want to collect a new category of consumer health data, or use it for a new purpose, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent before we do.

Where it comes from

  • Visit records: automatically from your browser when it requests a page from our web host.
  • Emails: from you, when you choose to write to us.

We don't buy or receive consumer health data from anyone else.

Who it's shared with

We don't have any affiliates. The categories of consumer health data that are shared, and the third parties that receive them, are:

  • Web hosting and security provider: GitHub, Inc. (part of Microsoft) receives visit records, because your browser requests every page from GitHub. Contact: GitHub privacy contact form.
  • Email forwarding provider: ImprovMX receives emails you send to our address and forwards them to our inbox. Contact: ImprovMX contact page.
  • Email provider: Google (Gmail) stores the emails you send us and our replies. Contact: Google privacy contact form.

We would disclose consumer health data to anyone else only if the law requires it. We never sell it and never use it for ads.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell consumer health data about you;
  • give you a copy of it, with a list of everyone we have shared it with and how to contact them;
  • delete it. We'll delete it from our records, including our email account and its deleted items, and tell ImprovMX, Google and GitHub about your request. We can't see or delete GitHub's visit records ourselves, so we'll pass your request to GitHub and tell you what we sent;
  • withdraw any consent you've given us.

Requests are free, up to twice in any 12 months. You don't need an account.

How to use your rights

Email support@homecircle.au from the email address your request is about, and say what you'd like us to do. Our health data request page has ready-made links and explains what to include. If we can't confirm the request is from you, we'll tell you why and may ask for a little more information. We won't ask for more than we need, and we'll use it only to check your request.

We'll reply within 45 days. If we need more time, we may take 45 more days once, and we'll tell you why within the first 45 days.

Appeals

If we refuse your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the word "Appeal", or by emailing support@homecircle.au with "Appeal" in the subject. We'll respond in writing within 45 days and explain what we did and why. If we deny your appeal, you can contact your state attorney general. For Washington: https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

Adults only

HomeCircle is for adults 18 and over and isn't directed to children.

Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new date.
