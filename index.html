Support for your recovery journey

HomeCircle is a free recovery support website for people in the United States. Breathe along with a guide, read plain-language guides and the 12 Steps, find US fellowships, and chat with Kai.

Meet Kai, your recovery buddy

Kai is the round button in the corner of every page. Tell Kai how you're feeling and it can suggest a breathing practice, a short meditation or a walk, or point you to a helpline. Replies are written in this browser from saved rules. No network and no AI service. Kai isn't AI, and isn't a person, counselor, doctor or sponsor. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
