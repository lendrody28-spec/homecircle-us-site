Health data requests and appeals

How to ask what consumer health data we hold about you, have it deleted, withdraw consent, or appeal our decision. Last updated 28 November 2026.

The site has no forms, so requests are made by email to support@homecircle.au. You don't need an account, and requests are free, up to twice in any 12 months. Read what we may hold in our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

1. Send your request

Each button opens a new email to us with the subject filled in.

Ask what you hold and for a copyAsk you to delete itWithdraw my consent

If the buttons don't open your email app, email support@homecircle.au yourself and put "Health data request" in the subject.

2. What to include

  • What you'd like us to do (confirm, copy, delete or withdraw consent).
  • Send it from the email address you used to write to us, if your request is about an email.
  • If your request is about GitHub's visit records, the approximate dates you visited. You can include your IP address if you know it, but you don't have to. We can't see those records ourselves, so we'll pass your request to GitHub and tell you what we sent.

Please don't send ID documents, health details or anything else we don't need.

3. What happens next

  • We'll check the request comes from you, usually by replying to the email address it came from. If we can't confirm it, we'll tell you why and may ask for a little more information.
  • We'll reply within 45 days. If we need more time, we may take 45 more days once, and we'll tell you why within the first 45 days.
  • If you ask us to delete, we'll delete what we hold and tell ImprovMX, Google and GitHub about your request.

4. Appeal a decision

If we refuse your request, reply to our answer with the word "Appeal", or use the button below. We'll respond in writing within 45 days and explain what we did and why. If we deny your appeal, you can contact your state attorney general. For Washington: https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.
