Kinds of meetings

Meetings may be in person, online (including video), or by phone. Some are open and some are closed. Other groups may use these words with their own rules. Check each meeting's listing to see if it's open or closed, and how you can attend.

In person

A meeting you go to in a room, or sometimes outdoors.

Online (video)

A meeting you join on a video call.

Phone or audio only

A meeting you join by telephone, or online with the camera off.

Open

Anyone can sit in and listen, including family and friends.

Closed

For people who want to stop drinking or using.

You can also read more about kinds of meetings on AA's own site: What to Expect at an A.A. Meeting, and attending a meeting online or by phone.
