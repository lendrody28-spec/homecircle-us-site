About this website

Do I need an account?

No. The website has no accounts and no sign-in, and everything on it is free.

Does the website collect my data?

No. There are no cookies, no ads, no trackers, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. Nothing you type or save is sent to HomeCircle.

What does the website save, and where?

Only what you choose to save: the breath-hold setting, Kai's chat and where you put Kai on the screen. These stay in this browser's local storage, on this device. Private browsing or clearing your browser data removes them.

Is Kai on the website?

Yes. Kai isn't AI, and isn't a person, counselor, doctor or sponsor. Replies are put together in your browser from saved rules. Kai makes no network calls. Kai looks for some words that may mean you're in danger and then shows 911 and 988, but it can miss things. If you're unsafe, call 911 or call or text 988 yourself. You can minimize Kai, drag it to the other side, or clear the chat.

Privacy and terms

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Site features that save anything (Kai's chat, where you put Kai, and the breath-hold setting) keep it only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to HomeCircle.
