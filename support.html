HomeCircle Support

Help with the HomeCircle website, and how to contact us.

In crisis or need help now? HomeCircle is not an emergency service. Call 911 in an emergency, or call or text 988. More helplines.

Contact us

Email: support@homecircle.au

Please include your device and a short description of the issue. This email is not a crisis line.

About this website

Do I need an account?

No. The website has no accounts and no sign-in, and everything on it is free.

Does the website collect my data?

No. There are no cookies, ads, trackers, analytics or third-party scripts or fonts, and nothing you save is sent to HomeCircle. Like any website, our web host, GitHub, logs visits. See the Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

What does the website save, and where?

Only what you choose to save: the "hide breath holds" setting. It stays in this browser's local storage, on this device. Private browsing or clearing your browser data removes it.

Privacy and terms

Read the Privacy Policy, Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Anything you save stays in this browser's local storage. Our web host logs visits.
