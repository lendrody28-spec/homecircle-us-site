Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy. Last updated 10 November 2026. HomeCircle is run by Luke Endrody (sole trader), Queensland, Australia, ABN 53 687 374 897. Contact: support@homecircle.au.

What we collect

We don't collect anything through the site itself. There are no accounts, cookies, ads, trackers or analytics, and we don't sell or share personal data or use it for ads. Our web host does keep technical records of visits, as explained under "Our web host".

What your browser saves

Some features save your choices in this browser's local storage, on this device only: the "hide breath holds" setting. We can't see them. To delete them, change the setting back or clear this site's data in your browser. Private browsing removes them when you close the window.

Our web host

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub (part of Microsoft). When you visit a page, your browser asks GitHub for it, and GitHub automatically receives standard request data, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver and secure the site. GitHub handles that data under its own privacy statement. We can't see or use those records. Because this is a recovery website, a visit itself may say something about your health; if that worries you, use private browsing or a network you trust.

If you email us

We get your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Our email is forwarded through ImprovMX to Gmail. Please don't send health details you'd rather keep private.

Consumer health data

See our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Adults only

HomeCircle is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or anyone under 18).

Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new date.
