Call 911 immediately if a life is in danger. Call or text 988 any time, 24/7, for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, including for a substance use crisis.

Helplines

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

For anyone in emotional distress, a suicidal crisis, or a substance use crisis. You don't have to be suicidal to reach out.

Call 988Text 988Chat online
More info
  • Free, confidential support from trained counselors across the US and its territories, 24/7.
  • Reasons to reach out include substance use, money worries, relationships, loneliness, and mental or physical illness.
  • Spanish: press 2, or text AYUDA to 988. Veterans: press 1, or text 838255. TTY: use your relay service or dial 711 then 988.

988lifeline.org

Crisis Text Line

For anyone who'd rather text: stress, anxiety, loneliness, self-harm or suicidal thoughts.

Text HOME to 741741
More info
  • Free, confidential support from a trained volunteer Crisis Counselor, 24/7, by text.
  • Your first message can say anything. A counselor usually joins within five minutes, sometimes longer when busy.
  • Volunteers aren't medical professionals and can't give medical advice.

crisistextline.org

SAMHSA's National Helpline

For people and families facing substance use or mental health issues who want treatment and support options.

Call 1-800-662-4357
More info
  • Free, confidential treatment referral and information, 24/7, 365 days a year, in English and Spanish.
  • Refers you to local treatment, support groups and community organizations. It doesn't provide counseling.
  • TTY: 1-800-487-4889. You can also text your 5-digit ZIP code to 435748 (HELP4U) for help near you (English only).
  • No insurance needed. They can refer you to state-funded or sliding-fee programs.

samhsa.gov

National Domestic Violence Hotline

For anyone experiencing abuse in a relationship, or worried about someone who is.

Call 1-800-799-7233Text START to 88788
More info
  • Free, confidential, 24/7. Advocates offer support, crisis intervention, safety planning and local referrals.
  • Live chat is on their website. If you're in immediate danger, call 911.
  • If someone may check your phone or browser, their site has its own quick-exit button.

thehotline.org

Gamblers Anonymous national hotline

For people who think they might have a gambling problem.

Call 855-222-5542
More info
  • A fellowship, not a professional service. Meetings are in person, online or by phone, with no dues or fees.
  • Hours for the hotline aren't published on the site.

gamblersanonymous.org

These are independent services. HomeCircle is not affiliated with them.

Al-Anon Family Groups

For people worried about someone with a drinking problem. Alateen is for teens.

Open al-anon.org
More info
  • Al-Anon members are family and friends who learn from others who have faced similar problems.
  • The site has self-assessment quizzes and meeting information.

al-anon.org

Nar-Anon Family Groups

For family and friends affected by someone else's drug addiction. Narateen is for teens.

Open nar-anon.org
More info
  • A Twelve-Step fellowship. The only requirement is a problem of addiction in a relative or friend. No dues or fees.
  • World Service Office: (800) 477-6291, Monday to Friday 8am to 2pm Pacific. This is an office line, not a crisis line.

nar-anon.org

Keep your details private

Don't share your full name, address, phone, bank details or passwords with anyone in HomeCircle or at meetings. Real friends and sponsors won't ask for money or private details. If someone does, tell your sponsor or a trusted person.

Code of conduct

Be kind. At meetings you find through HomeCircle, please respect that group's own guidelines, and don't record or share what people say. Questions or concerns? Email us, and we aim to reply within 24 hours. HomeCircle is support, not medical care.
